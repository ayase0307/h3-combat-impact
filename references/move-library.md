# 招式庫：讓模型有東西可演

## 0. 為什麼需要這個

模型的預設行為是生成**平均化的打鬥**——揮、擋、退。它不會自己發明具體動作。

寫 `he slashes at her` 會得到最通用的版本；寫 `he flips his wrist and rips the blade upward from below in a reverse cut` 才會得到「反手撩刀」。

**招式必須具名。抽象動詞 = 平均化動作 = 單調。**

### 每一招必須包含四層

| 層 | 內容 | 缺了會怎樣 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 位移 | 從哪到哪（騰空、貼地、橫移、俯衝） | 動作沒有空間感 |
| 2 身體機制 | 哪個部位發力（馬步、肩回轉、手腕翻轉） | 動作沒有重量 |
| 3 物理結果 | 氣流、塵土、裂紋、波紋 | 動作沒有打擊感 |
| 4 戰術目的 | 破站勢、破防、繞後、壓制 | 動作沒有方向 |

**第 4 層最常被忽略，卻決定動作「有沒有意義」。**

## A. 突進位移類

### 01 踏空縱躍飛衝｜踏風飛撲、高空襲殺

- **機制**：蹬地騰空 → 身體舒展前撲 → 從高處俯衝
- **H3**：`drives off the ground into a diving lunge, body stretched forward, robes streaming behind, descending on him from above`
- **關鍵詞**：`diving lunge`, `from above`, `robes streaming`
- **戰術**：搶制空、破高位防禦

### 06 飛燕低步穿林｜燕步穿花、低身突進

- **機制**：壓低重心 → 貼地疾步 → 上身起伏穿過防線
- **H3**：`drops low and sprints straight through his guard, feet skimming the floor, torso weaving, slipping under the blade line`
- **關鍵詞**：`drops low`, `sprints through`, `torso weaving`
- **戰術**：穿越、貼身、破解橫掃

### 03 疾步繞後偷襲｜幻影繞行、背身突襲

- **機制**：快速橫移迂回 → 留下殘影 → 繞至盲側
- **H3**：`slides sideways in three fast steps, leaving afterimages, circles behind him and closes in from his blind side`
- **關鍵詞**：`three fast steps`, `afterimages`, `blind side`
- **戰術**：繞後、打盲區

### 蹬柱變向（環境借力）

- **機制**：腳蹬柱子 → 改變方向 → 加速
- **H3**：`plants one foot on the column and kicks off it, changing direction in mid-air`
- **關鍵詞**：`kicks off the column`, `changes direction mid-air`
- **戰術**：在狹窄空間裡製造不可能的軌跡

---

## B. 下盤類

### 02 旋掃地腿絆擊｜地堂掃腿、貼地絆擊

- **機制**：單腿支撐 → 重心壓低 → 另一腿貼地橫掃
- **H3**：`plants one leg, drops his weight low, and sweeps the other leg flat across the floor in a fast arc, kicking up dust, aiming at the ankles`
- **關鍵詞**：`sweeps flat across the floor`, `kicking up dust`, `at the ankles`
- **戰術**：破站勢、逼對手跳

### 貼地翻滾受身｜滾地卸力

- **機制**：落地 → 順勢翻滾 → 起身還原
- **H3**：`hits the floor shoulder-first and rolls through it, coming up on one knee with her blade already turning`
- **關鍵詞**：`rolls through it`, `comes up on one knee`
- **戰術**：被擊飛後的回收，避免「躺著不動」

---

## C. 拳掌類

### 04 崩拳重勢轟擊｜裂山崩拳、重炮轟擊

- **機制**：馬步扎穩 → 肩膀回轉蓄力 → 全身貫力直拳
- **H3**：`sets his stance wide, winds his shoulder back, then drives a straight punch through with his whole body behind it; the air shudders at the impact`
- **關鍵詞**：`winds his shoulder back`, `whole body behind it`, `air shudders`
- **戰術**：硬破、正面壓制

### 05 流雲掌連環推｜流雲推掌、連環掌印

- **機制**：雙掌交替前推 → 每一下借前一下的勢 → 氣流成弧形波紋
- **H3**：`pushes both palms forward in an alternating chain, each strike riding the wake of the last, the air folding into curved ripples ahead of him`
- **關鍵詞**：`alternating chain`, `riding the wake`, `curved ripples`
- **戰術**：連續壓制、不給喘息

### 迴身背拳｜轉身鞭擊

- **機制**：背對對手 → 腰帶動 → 反手鞭出
- **H3**：`spins away from him and whips a backfist around without looking, the arm arriving before her head turns`
- **關鍵詞**：`whips a backfist around`, `without looking`, `before her head turns`
- **戰術**：被貼身時的脫困反擊

---

## D. 兵器類

### 07 反手撩刀斬｜回刃撩斬、反手破防

- **機制**：手臂反轉 → 刀鋒由下向上撩 → 月牙刀光
- **H3**：`flips his wrist and rips the blade upward from below in a reverse cut, the edge drawing a crescent of light that opens the guard`
- **關鍵詞**：`flips his wrist`, `rips upward from below`, `crescent of light`
- **戰術**：破防、從下路打開缺口

### 下劈掛｜力劈華山

- **機制**：刀舉過頭 → 全身下壓 → 垂直落點
- **H3**：`raises the blade overhead with both hands and brings it straight down, the edge biting into whatever is beneath it`
- **關鍵詞**：`overhead`, `straight down`, `biting into`
- **戰術**：終結、破格擋

### 突刺｜貫穿

- **機制**：重心前移 → 直線刺出 → 刀身不晃
- **H3**：`shifts his weight forward and drives the point straight out in a single line, the blade perfectly still along its length`
- **關鍵詞**：`drives the point straight out`, `perfectly still`
- **戰術**：距離差、破閃避

### 絞刃奪械｜纏絞卸兵

- **機制**：刀身相貼 → 旋轉絞壓 → 對方虎口鬆脫
- **H3**：`locks his blade against hers and twists, grinding the edge around until her grip breaks`
- **關鍵詞**：`locks blade against blade`, `twists`, `grip breaks`
- **戰術**：奪刀、結束戰鬥

## E. 落地與重擊類

### 08 千斤墜地落｜落地千斤、震地轟擊

- **機制**：高處重砸 → 單膝或雙拳觸地 → 地面裂紋 + 氣浪
- **H3**：`crashes down from above onto one knee, fists to the floor; the flagstones crack in a starburst and a ring of dust and pressure blows outward`
- **關鍵詞**：`crashes down`, `fists to the floor`, `crack in a starburst`
- **戰術**：群體壓制、氣勢

### 震地踏碎｜碎地踏步

- **機制**：單腳重踏 → 地面碎裂 → 對手失衡
- **H3**：`stamps one foot down hard enough to split the stone under it, and the shock knocks her balance sideways`
- **關鍵詞**：`splits the stone`, `knocks her balance`
- **戰術**：破站勢、製造空檔

---

## F. 防守與卸力類

### 撤步卸力｜退步化勁

- **機制**：後腳先退 → 上身保持 → 把力量引到側面
- **H3**：`gives ground with the blow instead of against it, letting the strike slide past her shoulder`
- **關鍵詞**：`gives ground with the blow`, `slide past her shoulder`
- **戰術**：被壓制時保命

### 袖纏格擋（布帛武器專用）

- **機制**：以布料纏繞兵器 → 借力偏移 → 順勢反制
- **H3**：`whips the sleeve around the blade and pulls it off line, the silk wrapping once before she yanks`
- **關鍵詞**：`whips the sleeve around`, `pulls it off line`, `yanks`
- **戰術**：以柔克剛、奪取主動

## G. 串接配方：三招一組

單招看起來像「擺姿勢」，三招連起來才是打鬥。**三招 ≈ 1.2–1.5 秒，每招 0.4–0.5 秒。**

### 追擊型（上→下→上）

```text
飛燕低步穿林 → 反手撩刀斬 → 千斤墜地落
drops low and sprints through his guard → flips his wrist and rips the blade upward
→ crashes down from above onto one knee
```

### 壓制型（連續同向）

```text
流雲掌連環推 ×3 → 崩拳重勢轟擊
three alternating palm strikes riding each other's wake → a straight punch with his whole body behind it
```

### 繞後型（位移→盲區→收）

```text
疾步繞後偷襲 → 迴身背拳 → 旋掃地腿絆擊
slides sideways leaving afterimages → whips a backfist around → sweeps flat across the floor
```

### 破防型（打開→奪取→終結）

```text
反手撩刀斬 → 絞刃奪械 → 震地踏碎
rips upward from below → locks blade against blade and twists → stamps the stone apart
```

**配方規則**：三招必須涵蓋**三個不同的空間層次**（上／中／下）。三招都在同一高度 = 單調。

## H. 翻譯規則（中文招式 → H3 英文）

| 規則 | 說明 |
|---|---|
| **招式名不進提示詞** | `踏空縱躍飛衝` 對模型是無意義字串，只翻「身體機制 + 方向」 |
| **動詞必須帶身體部位** | ❌ `attacks`　✅ `winds his shoulder back and drives a punch through` |
| **一招一句** | 不要用逗號把三招串成一句，模型會壓縮成一個動作 |
| **每招都要有物理結果** | 塵土、氣流、裂紋、火花——至少一個 |
| **方向要具體** | ❌ `from below`　✅ `from below, upward through his guard` |
| **不要用形容詞代替動作** | ❌ `a fierce attack`　✅ `a straight punch with his whole body behind it` |
